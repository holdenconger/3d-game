Dramatis Personae
PROMETHEUS, a Titan, chained to a cliff
HERMES, messenger of Zeus
CHORUS OF OCEANIDS, daughters of Oceanus
KRATOS (POWER), enforcer
ECHO, a mortal shepherd

[A high crag above the sea. PROMETHEUS is bound to the rock. The CHORUS stands in
tiers of the cliff, the wind carrying their voices. A pale sun struggles through
clouds. From below, a faint bell, then the soft tread of a mortal.]

CHORUS:
    The waves speak, and the stone replies.
    The sky is iron; the irons do not sleep.
    O Titan, we keep watch, we keep witness.

PROMETHEUS:
    Watch then, and learn. The gods forget;
    a mortal footstep remembers for them.

[ECHO climbs into view, a small satchel, eyes wide. He steadies himself at a
distance, the cliff's edge between fear and awe.]

ECHO:
    Great one, I followed the gulls. They screamed
    your name. Is it true you gave us fire?

PROMETHEUS:
    True. And the art that tames it. I am repaid
    with this metal tutor that teaches patience.

ECHO:
    I brought water. The spring was bitter,
    but it is all I had.

PROMETHEUS:
    Set it there. Your hands are warmer than mine.

[ECHO sets down a clay cup and steps back. The CHORUS murmurs, torn between pity
and warning.]

CHORUS:
    Child, do not anger the thunderer.
    Mercy is a spark; it draws the storm.

PROMETHEUS:
    Let Zeus hear. The storm already leans.
    Tell me, shepherd, how do mortals spend
    this gift? Do they keep it as a hearth,
    or fling it as a spear?

ECHO:
    Both. We learn to light the night,
    and we learn to burn our neighbors.
    My village has a forge now; the river
    runs red when its doors swing open.

PROMETHEUS:
    So knowledge is a blade that cuts its holder.
    I knew that. I still chose.

[A wingbeat, sharp as a knife. HERMES appears on a ledge above, bright with
authority. KRATOS follows, carrying the law like a shadow.]

HERMES:
    Insolent Titan, even bound you tutor
    the dust that crawls. By order of Zeus,
    cease this meddling or the cliff will learn
    a deeper lesson.

KRATOS:
    Speak if you will, but speak to the storm.
    Our hands do not tremble.

PROMETHEUS:
    Hermes, you wear your master's voice.
    Tell him my silence is not for sale.
    A future stands on my tongue, and it will
    not bow for a messenger.

HERMES:
    The word you hide will ruin you.
    The bolt already warms.

PROMETHEUS:
    Then let it. The ruin is a bridge
    when crossed by truth.

[ECHO takes a step forward, then stops, torn. He looks to the CHORUS, then to
PROMETHEUS.]

ECHO:
    Lord, if the gods are deaf,
    what do you want us to hear?

PROMETHEUS:
    That courage is a habit. That fire is not
    a gift but a duty. Keep asking. Keep building.
    When the sky speaks in thunder, answer
    with work that makes the world kinder.

CHORUS:
    We hear, and we carry it down the cliffs.
    We will not be silent.

HERMES:
    Enough. Mortal, descend. The sky grows sharp.

ECHO:
    I will go, but not to forget.

[ECHO bows once to PROMETHEUS, then climbs down. HERMES and KRATOS turn to leave,
but pause as the wind rises.]

PROMETHEUS:
    Go, messenger. Tell Zeus this too:
    a chain can bind a body, not a cause.

[Thunder rolls in the distance. The CHORUS lifts their arms as if to steady the
air. The scene fades with the sound of waves and a quiet, stubborn breathing.]
