Controls: WASD / Arrow keys (drive), Space (handbrake), R (reset), H (help), M (mute)

School Computer 3D Driving Game

Drive the car around the track. Avoid the cones and don't leave the play area.

  • W / ↑: accelerate
  • S / ↓: brake / reverse
  • A / ←: steer left
  • D / →: steer right
  • Space: handbrake (drift)
  • R: reset car
  • H: toggle help
  • M: mute/unmute
